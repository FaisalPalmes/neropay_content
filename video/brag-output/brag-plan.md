# Brag Plan: NeroPay Content Warehouse

## What is this app?
A static site that holds everything NeroPay publishes — every social post written, dated and drawn; three
YouTube series with every shot prompt and on-screen graphic, in English and Turkish; a backlog; a sixteen-week
calendar; and the compliance rails that bind all of it.

## The angle
One person makes five posts a week for a Manchester fintech, and none of it lives in a chat thread or a
Google Doc. It lives here, versioned, with the rules attached. The video is the site's own claim, shown, not
described: "Everything NeroPay publishes, in one place."

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The headline at full scale on the site's dark ground, Chivo 900, one line at a time:
"Everything NeroPay publishes," … "in one place." — with the kicker above it in Martian Mono.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The six hub cards arrive one by one — Social posts, YouTube series, Ideas, Assets, Calendar, Rails — each
  with a card-slide sound. This is section 00 of the site, "Where things are", recreated with its own copy.
- The stat strip counts up: 18 posts written · 12 videos, 3 series · 16 weeks mapped · 10 hard rails. The
  site computes these from its data files; the same numbers here.
- The EN / TR switch flips. "Every script in English and Turkish." One click sound on the flip.

## Outro / punchline
The last line of the site's own lede, verbatim: "what to post, when, and what they must never say." Then
the wordmark — Nero white, Pay yellow — and the tagline, then silence.

## User flow worth showing
none — landing-page only. The site is a reference, not an app; the "flow" is a person landing on the hub,
picking a page, and finding the post for the day. The six-page hub *is* the flow, so it is the centrepiece.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: a quiet internal-tool film — restraint, one idea per scene, the product's own words
- Interpretation: 4 scenes, slow reveals, soft crossfades, no lists, nothing shouted. Mixed case, generous
  spacing, Chivo for display, Source Serif 4 for the one sentence, Martian Mono for labels and numbers.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 21 seconds (630 frames at 30 fps)

## Visual identity (from the project)
The site is light by default with a dark theme it paints itself; the video uses the dark theme, because the
brand rule is Nero white / Pay yellow with yellow only as an accent on charcoal, and that is the theme where
the site meets it.
- Background: #0E0E10 (site dark `--bg`), surfaces #16161A / #1C1C21, rules #24242A
- Text: #F4F3F0 (`--ink`), secondary #B8B6B1, muted #7D7B84
- Accent: #F5C518 (`--yl`, the brand yellow), soft rgba(245,197,24,.11)
- Display font: Chivo (500 / 700 / 900) — local woff2
- Body font: Source Serif 4 (400) — local woff2; labels and numbers in Martian Mono (400 / 500)
- Strongest visual element: the hub of six page cards, and the stat strip that counts up

## Share copy (draft)
Introducing the NeroPay Content Warehouse: every post, script and publishing date in one place, with the
rails attached. Static HTML, no build step.

## Audio direction
- Role: sparse professional accents
- Music: **none — no licensed bed available in this session.** The bundled ende.app tracks carry no verified
  licence (upstream says so itself) and are not installed; the NeroPay CC0 bed in `video/library` is fetched
  from Freesound, which this container cannot reach. A bed can be added in the sandbox in a later pass.
- Music treatment: n/a
- Music cue guidance: none — sequential reveals sit on a fixed 0.5 s cadence instead of a beat grid
- Audio-reactive treatment: none
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched; Kenney CC0 from the skill's own library, low high-frequency risk only
- Audio-coupled moments: headline landing (soft impact), six cards arriving (card-slide, one each), stat strip
  landing (drop), the EN/TR switch flipping (click), the wordmark landing (bell, once)
- Restraint rule: nothing brighter than a rollover repeats; one bell in the whole piece; everything sits
  10-15 dB under where a voice would be

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 5.0s (0.0–5.0)
Dark ground. Kicker "CONTENT WAREHOUSE · SEPTEMBER 2026" fades up top-left in Martian Mono. The headline
rises in two lines, 0.4 s apart: "Everything NeroPay publishes," / "in one place." Chivo 900, 132 px. A thin
yellow rule draws under it. Holds until 4.3 s, then crossfades out.
Sequential/interaction: two lines, one after the other
Audio intent: arrival, weight, calm
Audio-coupled idea: soft impact as line two lands; nothing on line one
Music: none
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The six pages — 5.5s (4.6–10.1)
Section label "00 · WHERE THINGS ARE" in mono. Six cards slide up into a 3×2 grid, one every 0.5 s:
Social posts · YouTube series · Ideas · Assets · Calendar · Rails — each with its one-line description from
the site's hub in Source Serif. Cards are the site's dark surface with its rule colour and a
yellow label. Hold the full set 1.5 s, then crossfade.
Sequential/interaction: yes — six cards one by one, 0.5 s apart, each holds
Audio intent: things being put in their place
Audio-coupled idea: one card-slide per card; card-place on the last
Music: none
Transition mood: soft → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The numbers, and the switch — 6.5s (9.7–16.2)
Four figures count up together over 1.0 s in Chivo 900 with mono labels under them: 18 POSTS WRITTEN ·
12 VIDEOS, 3 SERIES · 16 WEEKS MAPPED · 10 HARD RAILS. They hold. At 13.0 s the figures dim to the side and an EN / TR toggle appears centre
with "Every script in English and Turkish." beneath it; the toggle flips from EN to TR at 13.8 s, the
sentence holds to 15.6 s.
Sequential/interaction: yes — a count-up, then a simulated toggle flip
Audio intent: precision, then one small satisfying click
Audio-coupled idea: a soft impact as the strip lands; a drop on the flip
Music: none
Transition mood: soft → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Outro — 5.0s (16.0–21.0)
The lede's last clause alone, Source Serif 4, 56 px, centred: "What to post, when, and what they must never
say." Holds 2 s. Crossfades to the wordmark — Nero white, Pay yellow, the site's `Nero<i>Pay</i> · Content`
lockup — landing with the one bell, tagline under it in mono: "EVERYTHING NEROPAY PUBLISHES, IN ONE PLACE."
Silence to the end.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: settle, then one resonant landing, then nothing
Audio-coupled idea: bell on the wordmark
Music: none
Transition mood: soft → end

**Music mood for this video:** none — no licensed bed in this session (see Audio direction)
**Audio summary:** five quiet, motion-matched accents and one bell; everything else is silence by design of
