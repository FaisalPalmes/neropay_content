# BRIEF — NeroPay App Store preview, iPhone

workflow: general-video
flow: standard

## Intent

The video in the first slot of the App Store product-page carousel: it autoplays when someone finds
the app in search, and it is built from the five screenshots Faisal exported, not from filmed footage.
Feature those screens as the video's own assets — this is a show-it-as-is piece, not an invented story.

## Decided (Faisal, 22 Sep 2026 — no questions outstanding)

- **Format** 886 × 1920 portrait, 30 fps, 26.0s. That is Apple's portrait app-preview size for the
  large iPhones and it is the same 0.4615 aspect as his 923 × 2000 screenshot export, so nothing is
  cropped or pillar-boxed. Apple's window is 15–30s.
- **Look** the screens move in 3D skewed perspective, never flat front-on. Layout shifts per screen,
  quick pushes in on a detail, smoother moves on the copy.
- **Signature** the Shutter (`BRAND-MOTION.md`): opens the film, cuts between every screen, closes it.
  Seam at 46.5°, never 45.
- **Audio** no voiceover. A bed and cues from the committed library, mastered to the house target.
- **No captions.**

## Assets

`assets/screens/s1…s5.webp` — Faisal's export, in his order. Everything else is derived by
`make-assets.sh`. The wordmark and the mark are placed artwork from `brand/`, never typeset.

## Rails that bite this one

No rate, no terminal price, no earnings claim, no real merchant data. The figures visible are the ones
inside his own demo screens. The Shutter is a picture and a sound and never becomes a line of copy —
no "opening", "unlocking" or "access" anywhere near it (`BRAND-MOTION.md` §1).
