## Three deliberate departures from Brief B

1. Brief B says *"three bonuses — between three and nine hundred pounds"*. That is a **total**, which the brief's rule 2
   bans. P3 says "a hundred to three hundred pounds each", which is per unit.
2. *"Nothing to unlock"* → "No tier to reach first." "Unlock" drifts toward the banned register (rail 1 / rail 2).
3. *"Not just the new three"* → "That's your whole network, including everyone you introduced before." The linter flagged the construction.
