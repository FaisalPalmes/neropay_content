Every post is final copy, posted as NeroPay, in the order it goes out. Under each one: the creative direction, a rough composition sketch, and the actual creative drawn and ready to download. Filter by channel, pillar or what's blocked.

01

The feed, week by week

Chronological, Monday to Sunday. Five a week when the plan is full: two Statement, one Street, one Product, one Partner, and a Merchant post every fortnight when consent lands. If a week is short, cut Product first, never Street.

02

How the creatives work

  • Every image is drawn, not designed. Charcoal ground, white type, yellow as the only accent, wordmark bottom-left. Stat cards at 1:1, carousel cards at 4:5, Reel covers at 9:16. Change the words in posts.js and the file redraws.
  • No money figure ever dominates an image. Percentages, counts and questions carry the design. That is the rule that got the profile restricted, and it applies to organic as much as paid.
  • Text-only posts have an optional quote card. Use it if the post gets reused as a carousel opener. Don't use it on the day — an image makes a said thing look produced.
  • Reel covers carry the AI tag where a generated presenter appears, so the disclosure is in frame from the first second.

Copy is final. Creative direction is a brief, not a spec — the sketches show composition and hierarchy, the drawn files are the default creative, and anything that improves on them is welcome as long as it stays inside the brand rules and the rails.
