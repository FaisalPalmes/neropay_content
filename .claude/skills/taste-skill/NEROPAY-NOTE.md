# taste-skill in this repo

Installed 19 Sep 2026 from https://github.com/leonxlnx/taste-skill (`skills/taste-skill/`, MIT; LICENSE beside this
file), at Faisal's ask. Four of that repo's thirteen skills are here: `taste-skill` (design-taste-frontend),
`redesign-skill`, `soft-skill` (high-end-visual-design) and `minimalist-skill`. The image-generation prompts
(imagegen-*, brandkit), the Codex-only image-to-code skill, stitch, brutalist, gpt-taste and v1 were left out.

Where they apply: the warehouse pages and the `social/` card templates, when Faisal asks for a design pass.
`SOCIAL-BRIEF.md` fixes the palette, the type and the furniture (light stage, Poppins plus one secondary, the kicker,
the wordmark bottom-left, yellow through frost); these skills decide layout, rhythm and what generic AI patterns to
avoid inside that. They never change a figure, a claim or a rail. The dark-mode protocol in taste-skill §8 applies to
the site's opt-in dark theme only, never to a social card.
