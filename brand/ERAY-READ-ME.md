# NeroPay Brand Kit

Logos, colours and social artwork for websites, company profiles and partner materials.

Open **preview.html** to explore the identity, view the artwork at a larger size and read the usage guidelines.

## Choose your files

| Folder | What you will find |
| --- | --- |
| logos | Transparent PNG wordmarks for light and dark backgrounds, plus black and white versions. Multiple sizes are supplied. |
| web | WebP logo versions for websites. |
| favicon | The diagonal N symbol in PNG and ICO formats, including an Apple touch icon. |
| social | Facebook covers and square company artwork. |
| linkedin | Profile and company cover images, plus square company logos. |
| seo | Dark sharing artwork at 1180 × 600 px, in JPG and PNG formats. |

## Logo versions

- **neropay-light**: charcoal nero and yellow pay with a fine contour. Use on white or very light backgrounds.
- **neropay-dark**: white nero and yellow pay. Use on dark charcoal backgrounds.
- **neropay-black** and **neropay-white**: single-colour versions.
- **Diagonal N**: use for favicons and compact square placements. The dark square company logo is suitable for profile areas that crop to a circle.

The transparent PNG backgrounds are intentional. View a logo on its intended background before placing it in a design.

## Keep it consistent

- Write **NeroPay** in ordinary text, with a capital N and P. The lowercase wordmark is supplied artwork, not a font to recreate.
- Keep clear space of at least half the height of the letter “o” around the wordmark.
- Preserve the aspect ratio. Do not stretch, rotate, recolour or add effects to the logo.
- Use the original files rather than screenshots or images copied from the preview.
- These are raster PNG/WebP assets, with JPG covers and ICO favicons. They are not editable vector logos or font files. Do not enlarge them beyond their native resolution; large-format printing needs suitable vector artwork.
