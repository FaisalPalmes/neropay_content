02

Seasonality worth knowing

  • September is the best month of the year. Schools back, students return, Christmas capacity planning peaks, and it's the last window for a POS migration before nobody retrains staff on a new till.
  • CPMs roughly double from October to December. Cold paid goes off in November. Organic social doesn't care, so it carries the load.
  • January is the sprint, not December. Cheapest month of the year for reach and merchants are actively reviewing costs — but the window for Muslim-owned merchants closes around 5 February for Ramadan. Write January's content in December.
  • Ramadan moves ~11 days earlier each year. From 2027 it eats the post-Christmas rebound window annually. Worth building into every future plan rather than rediscovering it.
