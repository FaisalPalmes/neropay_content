The backlog

What's next,
and what it would cost.

Every series NeroPay runs or could run, and the one-offs tied to a date. Each idea is judged by the engine rule: is it a by-product of the weekly video or a field day? Where it isn't, it says so, and it says "needs shoot" rather than pretending. Nothing here quotes a price or a rate.

01

Series

Three exist. Six are proposed. The first three are in production and don't change; everything after them is a decision for you.

02

Behind the Counter — the next four

Briefed, not scripted. Same shape as C1–C6: a question, four owners, a host close with a fact and a concession. Written when the first episodes have run and we know which owners land.

03

One-offs

Single videos tied to a date or a moment. Most are re-cuts of things already being made.

Ideas live in ideas.js; the next Behind the Counter briefs live with the series in calls.js. To propose more: three at a time, with the pillar, the channel, the hook, the creative, and which existing input it comes from. If it needs its own shoot, say so.
