# NeroConnect Platform Handbook — source of truth for the docs (internal; from a read-only walkthrough of the live product, 15 Sept 2026)

All names/IDs/figures below are illustrative. Product UI text, labels, behaviours and rules are real.

## 01 What NeroConnect is
NeroConnect is the platform layer of NeroPay. One business — the **platform** — creates and manages payment accounts for other businesses, its **connected merchants** (connected accounts), under its own brand. The platform sets what those merchants are charged, what they see in their dashboard, and which hardware they can buy; NeroPay remains the regulated processor underneath and keeps control of KYC, compliance and hard payout limits.

Three parties: NeroPay (processes payments, owns KYC and compliance decisions, sets hard caps no platform setting can exceed) · The platform (your NeroConnect account: creates merchants, sets their pricing, brands their dashboard and checkout, earns the margin between its own cost and what it charges) · Connected merchant (an individual business taking payments; sees a dashboard the platform has shaped, on a domain the platform controls).

| Area | Platform decides | NeroPay decides |
| Merchant pricing | Rate per payment channel, additional service fees | The protected base cost below which a fee cannot be set |
| Payout limits | May lower a merchant's limits | The hard cap; a platform value can never raise it |
| Account status | May deactivate an account from the platform side | KYC verification and compliance suspension |
| Branding | Logo, domain, store mode, dashboard menu visibility | Whether NeroPay-branded messaging is available at all |
| Hardware | Which catalogue items are visible, and their selling price | Supply cost, fulfilment and returns |

## 02 Screen map
NeroConnect is its own group in the left sidebar of the merchant dashboard, between NeroPOS and the Payment group. Nine destinations: NeroConnect Dashboard (attention queue, estate counts, platform wallet, month-to-date activity) · Connected Accounts (merchant register; search, filter, open account panel, create merchants) · Connect Orders (hardware/EPOS orders by connected merchants, payment-plan status) · Connect Reports (platform performance and ledger: volume, application fees, margin, reconciliation) · Connect Support (support plan tier, live chat hours, knowledge base, white-label support upgrade) · Connect Logs (payment activity across all connected accounts, per-transaction detail) · Connect API Logs (payments, IPN queue, webhook deliveries, raw API requests; read-only) · Connect Rewards (platform-owned reward programme for connected-account transaction milestones) · NeroConnect Settings (eight tabs: Branding, Store, Invoices, Notifications, Dashboard, Pricing, Custom domain, Support).

## 03 NeroConnect Dashboard
Landing screen. Answers: is anything waiting on me; how is the estate doing.
**Attention queue** — top block "Connected accounts needing attention". Runs independently of any date filter. Excludes closed cases and anything already under review, so an empty queue means nothing is outstanding. Three counters: Disputes (chargebacks where the merchant must supply a response before a deadline) · Transaction proof (payments where evidence has been requested) · Compliance (accounts with an open compliance check or missing information). Each row: merchant, connected account number, reason, case reference, amount, hard "respond by" timestamp in UK time. "Needs response" means the platform is expected to chase the merchant — NeroConnect will not answer a dispute on the merchant's behalf.
**Estate counters**: Total connected accounts (every merchant ever linked, whatever its state) · Active merchants (connected and able to process) · KYC pending (onboarding action outstanding) · New this month (sign-ups created in current calendar month).
**Onboarding pipeline**: three-state funnel Active / Onboarding pending / Not started with progress bars, plus new today, new this week, active terminals. "Manage accounts" jumps to Connected Accounts.
**Platform wallet**: GBP available balance (cleared platform funds) · Connect reserve balance (ring-fenced amount used to cover connected-account shortfalls; the buffer that keeps platform-paid processing working when the main balance is thin) · Current support plan (Free or Premium, link to plan management).
**Recent accounts and monthly activity**: newest merchants (business, contact email, country, status, created date) and three month-to-date figures: transaction count, processed volume, platform application fees earned (the platform's revenue line; reads zero whenever merchant pricing is set at cost).

## 04 Connected Accounts
Register of every merchant on the platform. Page header repeats estate counts and exposes the **platform account ID** — used when making API calls as the platform.
**Header banners**: states the current fee arrangement in plain language (who pays NeroPay processing fees; who pays monthly active-account and terminal fees). Below, up to three attention banners when relevant — onboarding stalled, disputes awaiting response, compliance information required — each with a jump-off button.
**Filters**: Search (business name, email, username, Merchant ID or NeroConnect Account ID) · Country (all, or one supported EU/UK market) · Onboarding (All · Connected · Pending · Not started) · Connection (All · Connected · Not connected).
**Columns**: Account ID (country flag + NeroConnect account ID e.g. NPa1b2c3d4e5_40118) · Business (trading name, username, last login — dash if never signed in) · Email · Created / Creator (timestamp, then platform entity and role that created it) · Account mode (Full active / Deactivated) · Channel (E-commerce · Terminal · No activity — the channel actually transacted on) · Attention (No action, or a stalled onboarding step with age and a Send reminder control) · Onboarding (Connected · Pending · Not started) · KYC (Verified / Unverified — set by NeroPay, not the platform) · Actions (View opens the account panel).
**Stalled onboarding**: Attention cell shows step and state, e.g. "Step 1 · In progress, 1 week ago" or "Step 1 · Stuck, 3 weeks ago". Stuck = contact the merchant directly; Send reminder only re-sends the automated prompt.

## 05 The connected account panel
Opening an account slides in a full-height panel. Header: business name, account ID, **View dashboard as connected account** (impersonation view for support).
**Balance strip**: Normal balance (merchant's payment wallet) · NeroBusiness balance (snapshot of linked financial account where one exists) · Reserve balance (funds held back on this merchant) · Debt balance (amount due; negative means the merchant owes NeroPay — usually an unfunded fee).
**Merchant login access**: Send login invitation (emails a set-up link) and Set password once (sets a password directly; usable a single time, after which normal reset flow). "Login setup available" badge when neither used yet.
**Tabs**:
- Add / pull funds — two mirrored forms, Send funds to connected account and Pull funds to platform: amount, currency (available balance inline), optional transfer group or note. How a platform settles a shortfall or recovers an overpayment.
- Merchant pricing — this account's own rates, overriding the platform default. Groups: Online Payment API (fixed, percent; online, API and store-order payments) · Card Terminal (fixed, percent; physical terminal) · Manual Keyed Card (fixed, percent; keyed and virtual-terminal) · Additional services (SMS fee · non-UK card extra fixed and percentage · extra payment method fixed and percent · instant payout fixed and percent · transaction refund fee; per event when used). Price below protected base is floored automatically; only genuinely funded positive margin credited. Legacy accounts whose pricing is all zeros inherit the platform default for that payment kind.
- Connected-account payout limits — eight fields: standard minimum, standard daily/transfer maximum, instant minimum, instant daily/transfer maximum, weekly standard maximum, weekly instant maximum, daily transfer count, NeroDisburse daily maximum. Each shows the effective value beside the NeroPay value. Blank = inherit. Panel marked "Hard cap protected": a platform value can only make a limit stricter.
- Rates & limits — read-only companion: what is actually in force after platform choices and NeroPay caps are applied. Shows display card rate, auth/fixed fee, stored bespoke rate, full pricing table incl. monthly account and terminal fees and weekly minimum, and the eight effective transfer limits.
- Funding access — whether this merchant sees the NeroFinance menu: Inherit platform default / Show / Hide. Menu visibility only; does not change eligibility or approval for finance.
- Identity verify — generates and stores an identity verification link: Create new link, Open latest link. Links expire quickly; generate immediately before sending. "KYC verified" badge shows current state.
**Panel footer** (every tab): API usage header for this merchant, and Connected account access with **Deactivate Connected Account**. A platform deactivation is recorded as such and can be reversed; it does not override a separate NeroPay compliance action.

## 06 Creating a connected merchant
"New connected merchant" opens a five-step wizard. Each step validates before the next unlocks; cannot skip or fill out of order. NeroFree is applied automatically; KYC still runs through NeroPay.
Step 1 Authorised person (first and last name, nationality, date of birth, account country, email, mobile; minimum age 13) · Step 2 Business (trading and legal identity) · Step 3 Addresses (registered and trading) · Step 4 Terms (agreement acceptance) · Step 5 Review (confirm and create).
Two country fields are deliberately separate: **Account country** must be a NeroPay-supported country and drives dial code and home market. **Nationality** is the authorised person's citizenship, from the full country list, and does not change the account's country.

## 07 Connect Orders
Hardware and EPOS orders placed by connected merchants through the platform store, with payment plans. Columns: order and date, connected merchant, total sale, supply cost, payment status, fulfilment, details. An Invoices button links to the invoice tab in settings. Fulfilment and return approvals are handled by NeroPay — a tracker, not a fulfilment console. Empty state when no merchant has bought hardware.

## 08 Connect Reports
Commercial view: what the estate traded, what the platform earned, what NeroPay charged, whether the ledger agrees with itself. Filters run across the whole page — from/to date, channel, single connected account — quick ranges today / last 7 days / this month / last month. Exports CSV and PDF. "Plan & fees" link opens the pricing summary.
**Monthly online & terminal activity**: three headline tiles — active e-commerce merchants, online payment volume, active card terminals — then a per-month table adding online payments count, active terminal merchants, terminal volume. **"Active" = at least one successful payment inside the selected dates.** A registered but untraded account or device does not count. Each merchant counted once per month, not once per website domain. Manual keyed payments and recurring invoices are excluded from the online figure but included in the broader e-commerce channel performance — which is why the two numbers legitimately differ. Period totals count unique merchants and readers, so need not equal the sum of monthly counts.
**Estate and ledger tiles**: Processed volume (value of successful connected-account payments in period) · Application fee generated (platform fee snapshots produced by those payments) · NCPA credited (actual platform application-fee credits recorded in ledger) · NCPF debited (actual NeroPay processing costs paid by the platform) · Net transaction margin (NCPA less NCPF, before monthly plan charges; also as % of processed volume) · Additional service margin (settled margin on SMS, card-origin, extra payment method, instant payout and refund pricing).
**NCPA / NCPF ledger trend**: three-series line chart — NCPA credited, NCPF debited, net margin — from actual ledger movements, not estimated pricing. Badge states current processing fee payer.
**Channel performance**: Terminal and e-commerce side by side: transaction count, volume, application fee, average sale; plus a fee-responsibility summary restating who pays processing fees, who pays monthly account and terminal fees, and any special pricing in force.
**Additional service economics**: platform earned, NeroPay base cost, platform keeps; per-service table: earned, base cost, platform margin, margin %, settled entry count. Only successfully settled ledger entries; a service row appears only once used. 0.00% margin = resold at exactly NeroPay's base cost.
**Ledger reconciliation**: NCPA, NCPF and NET restated as accounting records, with a status line confirming whether any application-fee difference was found in the latest rows. Monthly account, terminal and transfer charges are explicitly outside this calculation.
**Payment & platform fee report**: latest matching payments, each traced payment → fee → ledger settlement. Columns: date, connected account and merchant number, source reference, channel, volume, fee generated, NCPA, NCPF, net margin, ledger status (Reconciled or otherwise).

## 09 Connect Support
Two immediate routes — live chat during published UK hours, and the knowledge base — above a two-tier plan comparison.
**Free (default)**: connected account dashboard · application fee settings · Connect reports and reserve ledger. The platform's own team handles customer support, shipping and merchant operational questions.
**Premium (monthly)**: white-label support for connected-account tickets and live chat · white-label shipping support for store orders and hardware · white-label printing and terminal/EPOS device support · assistant phone support answering under the platform's support flow.
Premium is paid monthly by card or Direct Debit. If payment fails, Premium support is paused until payment is restored — not cancelled. On Free, connected-account orders are still fulfilled, but devices ship without custom white-label preparation unless the platform places the order itself.
(Do NOT print the Premium monthly price in public docs — say "shown on the Connect Support screen".)

## 10 Connect Logs
Payment activity across every connected account; attention queue repeated at top. Filters: free-text search, connected account, status, channel, date range. Results restricted to accounts owned by this platform.
**Status tiles** (ten): matching payments, succeeded, initiated, failed, cancelled, disputed, blocked, refunded, partially refunded, other/unknown. Collapsible "Connected-account payout fee records" holds historical entries with no platform allocation.
**Transaction detail** (View): built around **payment allocation** — Customer paid (original amount) · Connected kept (what reached the merchant, before card processing costs) · Platform fee received (the NCPA credit) · NeroPay costs (the NCPF debit) · Platform net margin (platform fee less NeroPay cost on this payment). A mode badge states whether the platform or the connected account is paying. In platform-paid mode the connected account is reduced by the application fee and NeroPay's processing fee is charged separately to the platform. Below: connected account identity, payment record, customer and order (name, contact, order reference, description), card and terminal (masked card, card profile, read method, location), linked references. Sensitive payment credentials never displayed; card numbers inside free-text order descriptions are redacted.

## 11 Connect API Logs
Read-only technical trace, four tabs. Standing reminder: **a successful payment and a delivered notification are different events.** HTTP 2xx confirms the receiving server acknowledged the message — not that the order was fulfilled. A checkout redirect alone is not proof of payment.
Tabs: Payments (payment records with customer, amount, status; filters: reference/ID, merchant ID, date range, payment status, customer name, email, phone, payment type, min/max amount) · IPN & notification queue (event name, notification type, delivery status, HTTP code, attempt count, acknowledgement time; filters: reference/ID, merchant ID, date range, delivery/HTTP status) · Webhook deliveries (outbound webhook attempts and responses) · API requests (method, endpoint, acting merchant and on-behalf-of merchant, HTTP code).
History up to 12 months; times recorded as the application saw them. Empty tab states "no recorded logs match these filters" — historical events cannot be shown if logging was not enabled at the time, different from nothing having happened. Each API request row shows the merchant pair as "Merchant #10042 → #10042" — how you confirm a call was scoped to a connected account rather than run as the platform.

## 12 Connect Rewards
Platform-owned incentive programme, separate from NeroPay's own rewards. Rewards earned against successful Card Terminal, Tap to Pay, Make Payment and manual keyed transactions. Two lists: **Published rewards** (catalogue merchants can see) and **Pending requests** (claims awaiting approval — approve only after confirming fulfilment). Creating a reward: reward name, successful transaction target, description of what the merchant receives. Own visibility switch: until enabled in Settings → Dashboard → Connected Account Rewards, a "Programme hidden" badge shows and connected accounts see nothing — rewards can be drafted before the switch is flipped.

## 13 NeroConnect Settings (eight tabs)
Most share one "Save NeroConnect settings" button at the foot of the page; notifications and payout limits (and merchant pricing, statement preferences) save separately — a common source of lost edits.
**Branding**: White-label login and registration URLs (links to share with merchants; active custom domain used automatically, otherwise the permanent NeroConnect platform URL) · Email/SMS sender handling (either NeroPay sends customer-facing messages as NeroPay, or available merchant-facing messages follow platform-branded handling and the webhook flow) · Support email, support phone, platform terms URL, platform privacy URL · Brand logo · switch "Show NeroPay logo on checkout page" — off for full white label, on where the NeroPay mark adds payment credibility.
**Store**: three mutually exclusive store modes — NeroPay original (standard NeroPay hardware store, checkout, thank-you page and branding) · White-label platform (platform branding, neutral product names, unbranded product images; network terms and fulfilment conditions still apply) · Hide store (no store links or terminal order prompts for connected accounts; use when the platform orders hardware itself). **Catalogue visibility** toggles each item across hardware (terminals, EPOS kit, self-service kiosk, KDS, Tap to Pay on Phone), accessories (docks, chargers, scales, scanners, receipt printers, cash drawers, paper) and a software section (web, booking, POS, trade and gym add-ons) with its own master switch. Hiding a terminal also hides its optional checkout add-on; hardware inside an EPOS bundle is not removed from the bundle; software visibility does not change subscriptions or dashboard permissions. **Store pricing**: selling price per item against a shown supply cost, live gross margin. Items can optionally offer monthly payments — total price, deposit, term of 6, 8, 12, 16, 18 or 24 months. Customer pays deposit at checkout and instalments monthly; a rounding adjustment may fall on the final payment. **The full supply cost is charged to the platform once, when the order is paid — not monthly — and the platform stays liable for it if the customer misses a payment.** **Discount codes** apply only to this platform's merchants, cannot be combined, reduce the selling price but never NeroPay's supply cost. **Seller and invoice details** optionally override registered business details for store invoicing only; a fixed, non-removable authorisation checkbox confirms the platform is the seller to its merchants and remains responsible for selling prices, tax details, payment plans and customer obligations. VAT: if no VAT status recorded, no VAT breakdown appears on issued invoices.
**Invoices**: register of invoices and credit notes NeroPay issues on the platform's behalf for sales to connected merchants. Separate from NeroPay's own supply invoices to the platform — keep registered business and tax details accurate because these documents use them.
**Notifications**: four platform email notifications, all off by default, independent of what merchants receive: Disputes needing a response (fires when a merchant must respond to a payment dispute; excludes won, lost and already-submitted cases) · Transaction refunds (a connected account submits a refund; excludes store returns; not confirmation of completion) · Compliance information required (a merchant must provide or replace compliance information; excludes information already under review) · Transaction proof required (evidence requested for a connected-account payment). Up to three recipients: one primary plus two optional CC or BCC. Enabling a notification may report existing open cases once; refund emails apply only to refunds created after enabling; saving never resends previously notified cases.
**Dashboard**: Statement reports — "Hide rates, costs and commission on statement reports": on = rates, deductions, commission, fee rows and fee-derived net totals disappear from connected-account statements and generated-statement lists; payment, refund and payout activity remains; existing statements must be regenerated; copies already downloaded cannot be recalled. Connected merchant dashboard — grid of hide switches for menu modules: apps marketplace, AI tools, card management, business banking, finance, POS, payment links, invoices, contracts, subscriptions, hosted forms, add money, virtual terminal, disputes, disbursement, games. Affects connected-account dashboards only, never the platform's own menu or standard NeroPay merchants. Connected Account Rewards — programme name and "Show to connected accounts" switch governing the Rewards screen.
**Pricing**: platform-wide defaults applied to newly created merchants. Two arrangements shown Read only because NeroPay owns them: processing fee payer, and monthly active account & terminal fee payer. Changing either requires contacting NeroPay; the page provides the request route and notes that a change to the monthly arrangement must be asked for explicitly alongside a change to the processing arrangement. Below: default merchant pricing by channel, default additional service pricing with each field's protected base printed beneath it, default payout limits (blank inherits NeroPay's limit; defaults can only make limits stricter), and a "Transaction History fee visibility" switch revealing NeroPay processing fees and original transaction references to the platform account and authorised platform staff only. Precedence stated on the page: account-specific choices in Connected Accounts → Pricing override platform defaults, but the account's admin limit is always the final hard cap.
**Custom domain**: partner-branded subdomain plus "Enable custom domain" switch. Two DNS records with copy buttons — a CNAME pointing the chosen host at the NeroPay endpoint (the record that activates the domain) and an optional TXT verification record for ownership checks. "Check DNS status" validates the current value without leaving the page; save the domain first if just changed. Once DNS and SSL are ready, verified login, registration and customer-facing NeroConnect links use the branded domain.
**Support**: status panel mirroring Connect Support: whether Premium Connect Support is active and what that means for device preparation. Connected-account badges and white-label preparation alerts surface in staff order management, staff task context and admin order emails wherever a merchant is linked to this platform.

## 14 Fee & money model
**Two fee-payer arrangements** — NeroPay processing fees: Platform account or Connected account. When the platform pays, it controls the spread between its NeroPay cost and what it charges merchants — more pricing freedom, more margin, more exposure. Monthly active account & terminal fees: connected account first, with platform balance fallback if the merchant cannot cover it.
**Platform protection**: platform-paid processing draws only on cleared platform funds and the dedicated Connect reserve. If those cannot cover a payment's NeroPay cost, that individual payment safely falls back to connected-account fee collection rather than creating an unfunded NeroPay balance. This is why the reserve balance matters operationally.
**Protected base and flooring**: every additional service has a NeroPay base cost printed under its field. Retail fee below it → base-cost floor applied automatically; the platform cannot sell a service at a loss through this screen; only funded positive margin is credited. A service priced exactly at base shows 0% margin in Connect Reports — correct, not broken.
**Hard caps**: payout limits run in one direction only. NeroPay sets the cap; platform defaults may lower it; per-account values may lower it further. Nothing in NeroConnect can raise a merchant above NeroPay's limit.
**Store economics**: hardware is the one place the platform carries genuine balance-sheet risk. Supply cost debited once at order payment, whether the merchant pays in full or over a term of up to 24 months; platform remains liable if the merchant stops paying. Discounts reduce the platform's revenue, never its cost.

## 15 Acting on behalf of a merchant (API) — MENTION ONLY, endpoints stay with Eray's API docs
NeroConnect exposes API v2 on behalf of each connected merchant. The platform authenticates as itself and names the target account in a header: `NeroPay-Account: <connected account ID>`. Every account panel shows the header for that merchant; Connected Accounts header shows the platform's own account ID. In Connect API Logs a correctly scoped call shows acting and target merchant both resolved on the request row. Treat IPN/webhook acknowledgement as delivery confirmation only; reconcile fulfilment against payment status, not a redirect.

## Partnership modes (from the older white label guide + fee update work — the two "modes" are the processing-fee-payer arrangement)
"Platform mode" = platform account pays NeroPay's processing fee; the platform bills its merchants itself and keeps the spread. "Connected mode" = the connected account pays NeroPay's processing fee directly; the platform's own fee is stacked on top and itemised. In BOTH modes the platform sets what its merchants are charged (this was previously documented wrongly). The arrangement is agreed with NeroPay at onboarding and shown read-only in Settings → Pricing. Dispute responses are always chased by the platform with its merchant; do not state who is financially liable for a lost dispute (unresolved). Do not print the safety/suspension threshold or the Premium support price as figures.
