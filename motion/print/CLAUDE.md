# Small Print — series brief

One series in the motion graphics style. **Read `motion/CLAUDE.md` first** — it carries the style,
the rails, the pipeline and the `figures.json` gate. This file is only what's particular to Small Print.

Briefed 15 September 2026 from the audience research in `motion.js` (MG02–MG06).

**Premise.** The law, the deadline and the rule a card machine touches that nobody selling one
mentions: the regulator's lease cap and trigger message, the Tips Act, the surcharge ban, chargeback
windows, Making Tax Digital. Each episode is one rule, its date, and the honest line at the end —
*if this doesn't apply to you, do nothing.*

**Why only NeroPay can.** The rules bind us too. Telling a merchant the regulator makes providers say
when they can leave is credible from a provider and nowhere else.

**Spec.** 30–50 seconds · white editorial ground (`#FBFAF7`), never charcoal · a stamp on every
date · one liquid-glass element per frame at most · primary 9:16, exported 16:9 / 4:5 / 1:1 · "we",
never Faisal · judged on saves and shares.

**The board.** Most episodes are boards — a calendar, a timeline, a receipt — because a rule with a
date has a shape you can walk along. The camera moves only between beats.
