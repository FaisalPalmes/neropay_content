# Partner programme — series brief

A series in the motion graphics style. **Read `motion/CLAUDE.md` first** for the style, the rails and
the gate. This file is only what's particular to this series.

**Premise.** The partner programme explained as an animated diagram — how a referral moves, who does
what, what a partner is actually signing up to. The thing a wholesaler, EPOS installer or accountant
would otherwise need a call to understand.

**Why it suits this style.** It's a process, not a person. A diagram that builds step by step explains
it better than a presenter describing it, and it re-renders when the programme changes.

**Channel.** LinkedIn is the partner channel — wholesalers, EPOS installers, accountants, trade
bodies. Not a merchant channel. 16:9 and 4:5 matter more here than 9:16.

## Blocked — and precisely on what

`/CLAUDE.md`: *"The partner incentive model is not signed off. No partner post carries figures;
L4 stays blocked."* `figures.json` carries `partner_incentive` as a blocked record, so the gate will
stop any episode that tries to state one.

**What that does and doesn't block.** It blocks every number: commission, share, tiers, per-referral
value, thresholds. Rail 10 also blocks partner commission and commercial terms outright, so this is
not simply waiting on a signature — even once the model is signed, the figures may still not be
publishable. Check both before assuming the block has lifted.

It does **not** block the mechanics: how a referral is passed, who owns the merchant relationship,
what happens at install, what support looks like, how long it takes. A figureless version explaining
the shape of the programme is publishable now and is probably the stronger video anyway — the numbers
are what a partner asks about on the call, not what makes them take it.

**Before writing one:** confirm with Faisal that a figureless version is wanted, and that the
mechanics described are accurate. Nothing here has been verified against the programme as it stands.
