**Why the tags carry one rate, not a different one per shop.** Faisal asked for tags like "£100 + 20%", "£200 + 30%", "£300 + 40%",
varying by shop. The bonus does vary by business, so each tag carries its own tier. The share rate does not: it is the partner's,
set by how many new active merchants they bring in that month, and it applies to the whole network at once. A different % per shop
would say something the programme does not do, so every tag shows the same rate and they climb together.

**Removed in v2 at Faisal's instruction.** The quiet-month section ("Fewer than three? No revenue share that month.") and with it
the brief's line "Your bonus is yours either way" (BRIEF.md v4 lists it as required; his instruction of 25 Sep drops it for this
video). The minimum of three still appears, said positively in the take and in the compliance super.

mock of the flow Faisal described, not screenshots of the app. The QR is a seeded pattern, not a real code. Swap in real screens
when they exist.
