## What changed in this version

- **"Tariff" is gone.** It's *rate* everywhere, in the scripts and on both statements.
- **Every line is shorter.** Average spoken shot is now **21 words**; the longest is 31. Nothing runs past 13 seconds and most sit between 8 and 11.
- **The copy hits harder.** *"They were quoted nought point five. They're paying more than double. And it was all there on the statement."* — that's the whole argument in nineteen words.
- **The global prompt now asks for expressive body language and hyperrealism.** Two new blocks: REALISM (skin texture, irregular blinking, small imperfections, precise lip sync, nothing morphing) and a rewritten MOVEMENT (micro-expressions, weight shifts, hands weighing things, small shrugs — engaged and warm, never stiff or theatrical).
- **Terminology stays.** Effective rate, authorisation, PCI, turnover, statement, minimum term. The words around them are what got simpler.
