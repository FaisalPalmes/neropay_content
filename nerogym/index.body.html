Three answers this waits on

  1. Is this a gym business or a software showcase? If the point is a reference customer for NeroGym the software, partnering with an existing independent gym (our software and door hardware free, shared content rights, a named case study) costs a fraction of this and carries no planning or lease risk. It has not been priced. It should be, before this is chosen.
  2. How much capital is available, and from where? £10–15k a month is a running budget. Opening is to depending on the tier, plus a deposit and first rent that are multiples of X. Equipment finance takes about £195k of the mid figure out and puts £4,000 a month back in.
  3. Before or after the investment round closes? A capital-hungry sister business started during a live £7m negotiation at a £40m valuation is a diligence question whichever entity holds it. Faisal's answer is inside Nero Panda Ltd; the case for a clean subsidiary and for waiting is on the sequence page.

Things to say plainly in the room

  • The price and the space promise are one decision. Ten square feet a member is a 500-member room. At realistic running costs a 500-member room needs a headline in the fifties. £34.99 at ten square feet does not close in any case on the model page.
  • Foundry gets there first, in the town centre, at £31.99, with Hammer Strength kit. Competing on price with a 27-site chain is not a plan. Competing on space, upkeep and a room people want to be in is one, and it costs more per member.
  • Eray's equipment thesis is half right. The chains are outspending everyone on kit (PureGym £177m capex in FY25). What they are bad at is repairing it quickly and duplicating the stations people queue for. That is cheaper to be better at than buying newer machines, and it is the commitment the concept is built on.
  • The creator angle needs the privacy answer before it is a feature. UK gyms have been restricting filming since 2024. Two screened creator zones and a filming ban on the main floor is the version members want. "The UK's first content creator gym" is a slogan that invites the wrong question.
  • The named risk is attention, not money. One engineer, a Germany launch in November, an unresolved partner fee discrepancy, a live round. Nothing in the model prices the hours this takes from those.
  • The research was read from search snippets. Direct page fetches were blocked in the session that gathered it. Every figure is dated and sourced; every estimate is marked. Before a lender or a landlord sees a number, someone opens the page.

Corrections to the September brief

Priors Way
The "Priors Way industrial estate" gym approval with an ~80-member cap is in Maidenhead, not Stockport, and the cap could not be verified from the local report. The point about planning conditions stands; the Stockport precedent does not.
PureGym recovery add-on
The £6–8 a month Recovery Zone trial at Manchester Urban Exchange could not be re-found this week. Carried as prior research, flagged.
Capital to open
September's £394,100 for Format B matches this brief's value tier without contingency and reserve. The mid tier is higher for five stated reasons on the costs page: ventilation, a changing block to Approved Document T, new kit, the lighting scheme, and holding a reserve. Each can be argued back; none should be assumed away.
Running costs
September's £13,594 all-in for Format B sits below this ledger's lean case plus rent. Energy at today's unit rates, remote monitoring of unstaffed hours, seven-day cleaning and a maintenance reserve sized to the repair promise are the difference. The ledger shows its working line by line.
Target age
16–35 in the brief; 27–40 in Stockport's demographics. Under-18s only in staffed hours; insurers want 18+ overnight.

Using this in the meeting

Open the premises page first and agree the must-haves; that is the conversation about where. Then the model page with X set to whatever the best live unit is asking, and move the price plan and running case until it either closes or it does not; that is the conversation about whether. If it closes, the sequence page is the plan and the costs page is what to get quotes on. If it does not, the partner option gets priced next week and this brief stays as the record of why.

Print works: each page carries its own tables, sources fold away, and the charcoal band prints.
