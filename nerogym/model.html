Break-even

Price plans

The recommendation is the Strength House plan. Three tiers, one room, the middle tier carrying most members. Prices include VAT because members pay VAT; the model strips it out.

Founder rate
First 250 members pay the off-peak price for 24/7 access, locked 24 months, no joining fee. Sold during the build. This is the discount the 86% realisation factor is paying for.
Joining fee
£0 in the pre-sale, £20 after. Foundry and the budget chains run at £0–£15; a small fee filters the 90-day quitters without pricing anyone out.
Minimum term
Rolling monthly with 30 days' notice, or a 12-month term at £5 less. Nothing longer: OFT v Ashbourne.
Student and off-peak
The off-peak tier is 09:00–16:00 and 21:00–06:00 weekdays plus all weekend, with student ID accepted at any hour on it. Fills the room when it is empty.
Corporate
15% off Core for ten or more staff at one employer, paid individually.
Add-ons and pay-per-use
Recovery £8 a month on Off-peak and Core (included in Plus). Massage chair £3 per 10 minutes. Creator zone free to book for every member. Day pass £12, week pass £30, guest £8 with a member.
PT licence
£300 a month per trainer, fixed, six trainers. Not a membership line, but the model's largest ancillary income.

The ramp and the downside

Thirty months from opening. Base case: 250 founders on day one, 45 gross joins a month until the room is at capacity, 4.5% monthly churn. Downside: 180 founders, 30 joins, 6% churn, 80% of headline achieved. The line is cumulative cash after all opening capital at the selected tier, so it starts at minus the capital and pays back where it crosses zero.

The verdict

The three answers this is waiting on

1. Gym business or software showcase? If showcase, price the partnership with an existing independent gym before choosing this. 2. How much capital, from where? The mid tier needs about plus the deposit and first rent; £10–15k a month is a running budget, not an opening one. 3. Before or after the investment round closes? A capital-hungry sister business during a live round is a diligence question whichever entity holds it.

Assumptions

Achieved price
86% of headline. The Gym Group achieves £22.14 against a £25.64 headline (H1 2026). Covers founder rates, promotions, freezes and arrears.
VAT
Memberships are standard-rated; one sixth of every payment is VAT and is stripped before anything else.
Churn
4.5% a month. The Gym Group's 18.5-month tenure implies 5.4%; the market runs 21–26 months. Front-loaded in reality (one in ten quit inside 90 days), so a flat rate flatters year one slightly.
Cost per gross join
£80 blended. UK Meta cost per fitness lead $28–$50 (2025–26) with 30–50% of leads joining after a visit; referrals and walk-ins bring the blend down. Marketing is therefore churn × members × £80 a month, never a fixed budget.
Collection
1.6% of membership revenue: GoCardless Standard 1% + 20p capped at £4.
Per-member consumables
£0.60 a month: wipes, paper, water, tag replacement.
Ancillary income
From the running-costs page: PT licences, vending, recovery, passes. Scaled down in proportion below 400 members in the ramp.
Fixed costs
The running-costs ledger for the chosen case, excluding the marketing row, plus X, plus service charge and rates as listed, plus equipment finance when toggled.
Capital
The one-off ledger total for the chosen tier including contingency and reserve. Deposit and first rent excluded (they are multiples of X).
Not modelled
Corporation tax, VAT cash-flow timing, owner drawings, price rises, a second site, and any value to NeroPay from the software reference. Interest on any borrowing beyond the equipment toggle.
