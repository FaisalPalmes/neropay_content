How to read the sequence

Phases overlap. Equipment with a twelve-week lead is ordered while the build is still being tendered; the software goes on the door six weeks before opening; the pre-sale starts the day the hoarding goes up. What does not move is the order of the gates: no design money before a unit is secured, no build money before permissions, no equipment deposit before the build programme is fixed, no opening before the compliance list is signed off.

Figures are ex VAT and mid tier unless a phase says otherwise. The value and premium columns are on the costs page.

Decide and set up Month 0 · gate

Nothing is spent until Eray has answered three questions in writing: gym business or software showcase; how much capital and from where; before or after the investment round. If the answer to the first is "showcase", the partner-with-an-existing-gym option gets priced and this sequence stops. If it is "gym", the entity question follows immediately: a subsidiary of Nero Panda Ltd keeps money and records clean during a live round; a division inside it does not.

  • Board minute recording the decision, the capital ceiling and the entity.
  • Company formation if a subsidiary; VAT registration; payroll scheme.
  • Trade mark search on NEROGYM and NERO in classes 41, 9 and 44, then file. "Nero" is a crowded register.
  • Name a general manager candidate now, even informally. The gyms that work are run by someone who is there.

Find and secure the premises Months 0–3 · gate

The premises page is the checklist. The work here is phone calls and site visits, then the professional checks that turn an agent's brochure into facts: lawful use, floor loading, asbestos, power, drainage. Agree heads of terms with the rent-free period and alterations consent written in, then instruct the solicitor. The deposit and first quarter's rent are real cash at this point and are the only premises costs not in the totals, because they are multiples of X.

  • Ask every agent the same question first: what is the unit's lawful planning use, and is there a CLOPUD or planning history to prove it.
  • Electricity North West supply enquiry on the shortlist (quotes in five working days).
  • Structural engineer on the slab; asbestos refurbishment survey if pre-2000.
  • Heads of terms → solicitor → exchange. Budget three months from first viewing.

Design and permissions Months 2–5

A designer who has done gyms turns the concept page into a layout, a finishes schedule and tender drawings. M&E design makes the ventilation and electrical quotes comparable. The lighting designer is the one professional fee that is a selling point rather than a compliance cost. Building Regulations full plans go in here, planning only if the unit needs a change of use, and the fire strategy is checked against the access-control design on paper, because installers get the means-of-escape rule wrong and it only surfaces in an investigation.

  • Layout at 50% strength machines, 30% free weights, 20% cardio; two of everything that queues; two creator zones screened from the floor; changing block on the existing drainage.
  • Equipment order placed at the end of this phase. Hammer Strength and Life Fitness run twelve weeks; Primal and Origin hold UK stock.
  • Three like-for-like quotes for the build; one from a gym specialist, two from good local contractors under the designer.

Build Months 5–9

Sixteen to eighteen weeks on site for a 465 m² fit-out with a new changing block. The two lines that decide whether this phase lands on budget are the ventilation and heating system and the drainage for the showers, which is why both are surveyed in Phase 1. Flooring goes down last, after the electrical second fix and before the equipment arrives. Signage needs advertisement consent if it is illuminated; apply in Phase 2.

  • Weeks 1–3 strip-out, slab repairs, drainage. Weeks 3–8 partitions, changing block first fix, electrical and data first fix, ventilation ductwork. Weeks 8–13 tiling, cubicles, lockers, lighting, decoration, joinery, acoustics. Weeks 13–16 flooring, mirrors, signage, second fix, testing and certificates.
  • Site visit every Friday with the designer. Photograph everything: it is the build diary the pre-sale runs on.
  • EICR, fire alarm and emergency lighting certificates handed over before practical completion, not after.

Fit and equip Months 8–10

Equipment lands in the last fortnight of the build and takes six to eight days with two fitters and a forklift. Then everything that sits on top of the building: the reader door and its fail-safe lock, cameras to every training area (insurers require it), the intruder alarm, the network, the screens, the sound, the furniture, the creator zones, the recovery kit, the vending, and the NFC and QR tags on every station.

  • Access control tested against the fire alarm: every exit opens with one push when the alarm sounds and when the power fails.
  • Defibrillator mounted where it can be reached in under three minutes from any point, signed, and registered on The Circuit.
  • Tags encoded, locked read-only, and mapped to stations in the app before the first member scans one.

Comply, insure, staff Months 8–10 · gate

The list of things that have to exist before the first member walks in. Several carry unlimited fines. The fire risk assessment is written on the finished room, the legionella scheme on the finished hot-water system, the insurance on the finished camera plan. Staff are hired to start two to four weeks before opening so the induction happens in the room. The membership terms and the PT agreement are drafted by the right kind of solicitor, because the templates in circulation cite withdrawn guidance.

  • Fire risk assessment recorded in full · legionella risk assessment and written scheme · PAT and certificates · music licence · ICO fee · food registration for the vending, 28 days ahead.
  • Insurance bound with the 24/7 conditions met: CCTV to every area, panic buttons or lanyards, photo ID at join, 18+ in unstaffed hours, AED on site.
  • General manager starts two months out, duty manager one month, hosts and cleaner two weeks. First Aid at Work for three people, AED training for all.
  • Emergency plan written against the Henry Best inquest findings: who is "staffed", how the alarm is used, where the AED is, who meets the paramedics at the door.

Software and pre-sale Months 7–10

Phase 1 of the Nero app is exactly what the software product needs to sell to anyone: join, Direct Debit mandate, QR entry, bookings, cancel in two taps. It is on the door six weeks before opening with a plain fob reader in the cupboard as fallback. The pre-sale starts when the hoarding goes up in month 7: a founder price for the first 250, locked for 24 months, sold from the build diary and a Saturday walk-round in the shell. The room has to be a third full on day one or the ramp on the model page does not hold.

  • Hoarding wrap and banners on the fascia the week the lease starts. Leaflets to the postcode sectors within a ten-minute drive. Paid social from month 8.
  • Ten local creators signed to the programme; their first content is the empty room and the racks arriving.
  • Corporate outreach to the estate's employers with a 15% staff rate: the daytime members.

Contingency and cash reserve Held back · gate

Ten percent on the build, fit and equipment for what the surveys did not find. And six months of running costs before rent, because the ramp reaches its worst cash position around month four and a gym that is solvent on paper and out of cash in April is closed. These two lines are why the capital figure on the overview is higher than the September model's. They are not optional and they are not the first thing to cut.

Opening week

  1. Soft open to founders only for five days. Every fault logged in the app and fixed before the public open; the 48-hour promise starts here.
  2. Public open on a Saturday morning with the creator programme in the room and the signature wall lit.
  3. Attendance dashboard live from day one: the members who have not been in for 14 days get a message in week three, because cancellation lags the last visit by about five months and this is the only lever that moves churn.
  4. First maintenance visit booked for week six. First insurer review at month three. First price review at month twelve, with the founder rate honoured to month 24.
