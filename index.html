01

What social is actually for

NeroPay wins merchants at the counter, through partners, and on the phone. Social's job is narrower and more useful than "generate leads":

  • Credibility after contact. A card gets left in a Rusholme takeaway. That evening the owner searches NeroPay. What they find decides whether the follow-up call gets answered.
  • Partner recruitment. Wholesalers, EPOS installers and accountants are reachable on LinkedIn. Merchants aren't. This is the one place social can start a relationship rather than support one.
  • Manufacturing shopping-around behaviour. 42% of UK merchants haven't switched or considered switching in two years. They're not trapped — they're not looking.

What it is not for: direct response. If a post produces a merchant, good. Don't judge the channel on it and don't restructure the plan to chase it.

How one person makes five posts a week

Two inputs already committed to: one long-form video a week and one field day. From those come three vertical cuts, a carousel, a LinkedIn write-up, street photos and a field note.

The rule that keeps this alive: nothing is made specially for social. If a post idea needs its own shoot, script or research, it doesn't happen. Everything is a by-product of work that was happening anyway.

Batch it. One afternoon a week, all five written and scheduled. Publishing daily is the fastest route to publishing nothing by November.

05

Adding to this site

Content is separated from presentation so nobody has to touch HTML to add a post.

  • A new social post — copy an object in posts.js, change the fields, save. It appears automatically, filters included.
  • A new video — videos.js is generated from generation-pack.md. Keep the markdown as the source of truth.
  • Anything else — the pages are plain HTML with one shared stylesheet.

Push to main and Vercel redeploys. See README.md.
